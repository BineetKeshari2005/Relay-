### Mandatory Safety Policies

1. **Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)**:
   - Before removing condenser fan grilles or accessing high-voltage electrical compartments, the main rooftop disconnect MUST be opened, locked, and tagged.
   - Zero-energy state must be verified using a category-rated digital multimeter.

2. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**:
   - Technicians must wear safety glasses with side shields and puncture-resistant cryogenic/leather gloves when connecting or disconnecting pressure hoses.
   - High-decibel hearing protection is required when operating compressors at peak load.

3. **Pressure Boundary Rules**:
   - Never heat a pressurized refrigerant component with an open flame.
   - If operating pressure exceeds 250 PSI, technicians must immediately de-energize the compressor and evacuate the immediate equipment perimeter.
   - Pressure relief valves must NEVER be capped, plugged, or bypassed.

4. **Escalation Protocol**:
   - If emergency pressure relief valves have vented, or if structural line rupture is suspected, report the incident immediately to the site safety manager and halt all maintenance.
